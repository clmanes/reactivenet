The app is written in Italian, for Italian schools and their terminology.
Everything below describes what it does.

## The first thing to say

This app handles **health data about minors** — the highest-risk category a school
holds. Protection is not a garnish here; it decided the shape of the program.

- **Nothing leaves the device.** It all lives in the browser.
- **No diagnosis, in any field.** Of the certification it records the issuing body, the
  date and the expiry. There is no report field and no medical attachment: that is a
  construction choice, not an omission.
- **The AI forms no hypothesis about the pupil.** The only two AI directives present
  rewrite text a teacher has already written, and search the bank of phrasings.
- **School-wide summaries carry no names.** They count; they do not list.

## What it does

**Templates are rows, not code.** The plan's observation dimensions for the four school
stages, and the personalised-plan sections, are edited from a page of the app, each
with its guidance text and its own version — so an approved document stays readable and
reprintable with the template it was written under.

**The per-subject contribution is a page of its own**, holding only the table each
subject teacher fills in for their own subject, and no clinical-functional picture. It
is built to be shared on its own.

**The operational extract for the class council** is one page with the measures, the
tools and the assessment criteria, and nothing else.

**Objectives and reviews** sit on a board you drag by outcome, and close with the
proposal of resources for next year — the row that feeds the staffing request.

**The bank of phrasings** cuts down typing, and the search is **semantic**: a sentence
in your own words finds the objective about attention even without sharing a word with
it. The index is built on the device and never leaves it.

**A check flags similarity.** Two sections for different pupils sharing more than 60
per cent of their words are probably the same section pasted twice — and a copied plan
is a plan that can be challenged. It flags; it does not block.

## What it does not do, and says so

It archives no medical reports, signs nothing digitally — it records that a signature
was collected, by whom and how — and does not lock a document after approval. Per-role
access control is not in the document: forced separation is the platform's shared
spaces, and must be decided before the app is used on real data.
