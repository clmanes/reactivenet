## 2. What the platform is

ReactiveNET lets you describe an application in a text document and run it **in
the browser**. Its parts:

- **the application**, which interprets documents and keeps data in the browser;
- **sharing links**, long (the app travels inside the address) or short (the app
  is deposited, encrypted, on one of our servers);
- **shared spaces**, end-to-end encrypted synchronisation between several
  participants, with roles;
- **the open-data service**, read-only querying of Italian public datasets;
- **the MCP server**, which lets an AI assistant write and check an app.

**holds no copy** of them and is unable to recover them.

It follows, bluntly: clearing the site's data in the browser, using private
browsing, uninstalling the browser or losing the device **means losing any app

The provider acquires no rights over user content. Over content deposited for
synchronisation or for a short link it acquires only the technical ability to

## 5. Accounts and shared spaces

The account for shared spaces is pseudonymous: no email address is required.

- Users are responsible for keeping their credentials confidential and for
  activity carried out with them.
- **The password is the key to the encrypted content.** Anyone who loses it
  without the recovery code loses access to their shared spaces and their
  content. There is no reset procedure, because there is no way to offer one
  without opening a back door in the encryption.
- Whoever creates a space administers it, invites others and assigns roles.
  Revoking a role prevents reading what happens **after** the revocation: what a
  member has already read or copied cannot be called back.
- A read-only participant may change their own local copy; their changes are not
  sent to the others.

## 7. Sharing links

Whoever generates a link shares the app with **anyone who comes to hold it**: a
link is not an access control. The long link carries the app in the address and
touches no server; the short link deposits the app, encrypted, on our systems,
with the key in the address fragment, which does not reach us.

Short links are **automatically deleted 120 days after they were last opened**.
A link is not an archive.
